## 4. Activation, the internet, and your privacy

Activating a key sends the key and the name of this product to Gumroad, the
store that processes the sale, so it can confirm the purchase. Gumroad's reply

**That is the only network request the Software ever makes.** It does not
collect analytics, does not report what you render, does not transmit your
audio, your file names, your project contents or any personal data, and has no
other server behind it.

After activation the Software re-checks the key roughly every 14 days and
keeps working offline in between, for up to 60 days without a successful
check. It is designed so that a studio with no internet connection is not
locked out.

A computer stores a small record of the activation locally. Pressing **Remove
the license from this computer** deletes it.
